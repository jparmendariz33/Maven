## Resumen del negocio
Becle, S.A.B. de C.V. manufactures and distributes spirits and other distilled beverages in Mexico, the United States, Canada, and internationally. The company offers tequila, whiskey, vodka, rum, gin, mezcal, ready-to-drink, and non-alcoholic products. It sells its products under the Bushmills, Tequila 1800, Jose Cuervo Especial, Jose Cuervo Tradicional, Jose Cuervo Reserva de la Familia, Gran Centenario, Maestro Dobel, Bushmills Whiskey, Stranahan's Whiskey, Proper No. Twelve Whiskey, Great Jones Whiskey, The Sexton Whiskey, Pendleton Whiskey, Tincup Whiskey, Hangar 1 vodka, Three Olives Vodka, Kraken Rum, Owney's Rum, Matusalem Rum, Boodles Gin, Mezcal Creyente, 400 Conejos, Santín, Kraken Rum and Cola, Authentic Margarita, Golden Margarita, Jose Cuervo Paloma, 1800 Ultimate Margarita, Jose Cuervo Margarita Mix, and Sangrita Viuda de Sanchez brands.
